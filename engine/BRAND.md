# Sundae brand rules for creative edits

Every change must keep the asset inside these rules. They condense Sundae's Creative Guidelines (2022).

## Hard rules
1. **No red text, anywhere.** Sundae red `#DB3D55` is never a text color. Red appears only as the wordmark or "S" icon, filled shapes, accent bars, dots and highlight boxes (with large bold white text inside).
2. **Red stays represented** on every canvas (the red wordmark is enough). Never remove the wordmark.
3. **Copy is `#4A4A4A`** on light backgrounds. White text only on blue `#1C51A0`, on red (large text only), on `#4A4A4A` fills, or inside a box on a photo. Blue is for buttons, chips and links.
4. **Fonts: Merriweather and Lato only.** Merriweather for headlines and callouts, Lato for everything else (Lato 900 for a headline in a tight space). Never all caps, except eyebrows via CSS.
5. **Palette only:** red `#DB3D55`, blue `#1C51A0`, ink `#4A4A4A`, pink `#F4CCCC`, mist `#F4F7F9`, gray `#E6E6E6`, slate `#C9D2E0`, white. No gradients between other colors, no black or dark backgrounds, no gold, cream, neon, glow or film grain.
6. **No superlatives** in our copy (best, highest, top, #1, fastest, largest, leading, premier, ultimate, unbeatable, guaranteed, "most" as a claim). No earnings or income claims, ever. Verbatim customer reviews and quotes stay exactly as written.
7. **Offer metrics are averages:** an offer count, price uplift or days-to-close must read "An average of X …" or carry an asterisk with a footnote saying the number is an average. Never present one as a promise, and never remove the "average" wording or its footnote.
8. **AP style:** "7 p.m." (no ":00"), "Thursday, Oct. 8", "Oct. 8, 2026"; abbreviate Jan., Feb., Aug., Sept., Oct., Nov., Dec. only with a date; spell out one through nine; "and" not "&" in running copy (formal names such as "Private Dinner & Dialogue" keep it); no Oxford comma.
9. **Logo:** red wordmark on white, mist, pink or slate; white wordmark on red, blue or a solid #4A4A4A field; on a photo it sits on a solid white or red field. Modest size (about 150–200 px wide on a 1080 canvas, 120–150 px on a flyer), clear space of at least the height of the "u" on every side, never the biggest element, never stretched or recolored.
10. **Contrast and size:** WCAG AA (4.5:1 normal text, 3:1 large). Legal, compliance and footnote copy is never smaller than the smallest other copy on the asset. Minimum sizes: 1080-wide social body 30px, eyebrow 22px, headline 64px; Stories and Reels body 36px, headline 80px; flyers (816×1056) body 15px, smallest 12px, headline 40px; landscape video body 34px, headline 72px.
11. **Accuracy:** no new facts, claims, numbers, photos or reviews. Dates, times, venues, addresses, phone numbers, names, prices and URLs keep their values unless the change was approved. Real photos stay real; never add stock, AI images or illustrations.

## Look
White space first. Headlines in Merriweather `#4A4A4A` with a red accent bar to the left. Text over photos sits in highlight boxes (white box with ink text; red box with large white text; blue chip with white text for dates and times). Photos are clean rectangles or full bleed and are never darkened. Shapes, sparingly: a red rectangle with one large rounded corner, a slate circle, pills, a 4×4 blue dot grid.

## Voice
Expert, empathetic, trusted. Kind, uncomplicated, direct. Tighten hype; never add claims.
