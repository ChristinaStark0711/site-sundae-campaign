## 2. Who gets a seat (the qualification bar)

A seat is a sales opportunity. Fill the room with people who could become members, not with
spectators. Approve a request when **at least three** of these are true:

| Signal | Approve when | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Role | Owner, founder, principal or acquisitions lead of an investing company | The decision-maker on a membership |
| Volume | 6+ deals closed in the last 12 months (or 3+ with a funded team) | Proven operating experience (Sundae's own fit list) |
| Model | Wholesale, fix-and-flip, buy-and-hold acquisitions or a team doing several | The Sundae Engine fits acquisition businesses |
| Market | Fresno County, Clovis, Madera and the surrounding Central Valley | Territory relevance for this dinner |
| Marketing spend | $5,000+ a month on acquisition marketing today | Can deploy the system |
| Intent | "Scaling in the next six to 12 months" | Timeline for a decision |

Everyone else gets a polite "we'll invite you to the next one" plus the Sundae Marketplace link.
Nobody is left without a reply.

**The RSVP link decides how this works.** The Manhattan Beach RSVP page was built for that
dinner only and stays as it is. For Fresno the options are a form you supply (Google Form,
GoHighLevel form, Calendly) or a new RSVP page, quoted as its own job. Whichever it is, ask the
six questions above, no sign-in wall, and route every request into GoHighLevel for scoring.

## 5. Confirmation and show-rate system

Free dinners commonly lose 30%-40% of approved guests to no-shows (the Manhattan Beach plan's
working figure, an assumption).

1. **Instant reply (under five minutes):** SHAI chat or SMS agent (AI-disclosed) thanks them and
   says a seat decision comes within two hours.
2. **Approval (under two hours):** email + SMS with a calendar invite, the venue's parking note
   and "reply YES to confirm."
3. **Personal call (Monday, Nov. 16, and Tuesday, Nov. 17):** a human from Victoria's team, or the SHAI
   voice agent with AI disclosure, confirms attendance and asks "what's the main thing you want
   from the night?" That answer goes into Josh's prep and the seating.
4. **Reminders:** 24 hours, three hours and 30 minutes before the start, with the address and a
   map link.
5. **Waitlist:** keep 15%-20% overbook headroom; release waitlist seats Tuesday, Nov. 17, at noon.
6. **Plus-ones:** a business partner (not a spouse or friend) may come; they request a seat too.

Target: **70%+ of approved seats show** (base case: 71 approved → about 49 seated).
