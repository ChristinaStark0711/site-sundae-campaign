## 4. Turning registrations into attendance

Evening events commonly lose 30%-40% of registrants (the Manhattan Beach plan's working figure, an assumption).
1. **Confirmation:** the HubSpot registration sends the location; check with Sundae what that email says.
2. **Reminders:** one week, one day and three hours before 6 p.m., each with the address once Sundae shares it.
3. **Personal touches:** Victoria's team calls or texts the top-50 registrants on Monday, Nov. 16, and Tuesday, Nov. 17.
4. **Plus-ones:** registrants can forward the link to a business partner.
