# Event Plan — Paid Ads: save the date, then a two-week seat sprint

**Budget:** $5,000 base ($3,000 conservative · $8,000 aggressive). These are the Manhattan Beach
figures, used here as a labeled assumption until you set a Fresno budget.\
**Flight:** Phase 1 (save the date) from Monday, Oct. 26 until the time, venue and RSVP link are
confirmed (target Friday, Oct. 30); Phase 2 (seat sprint) from confirmation (target Monday,
Nov. 2) to Friday, Nov. 13, with retargeting until Monday, Nov. 16, at noon.\
**Goal:** about 80 qualified seat requests from paid; organic covers the rest (plan 01).\
**Conversion event:** Phase 1 = **interest captured** (an in-platform lead form or a booked intro
call); Phase 2 = **qualified seat request**, sent to Meta (Pixel + Conversions API), LinkedIn
(Insight Tag) and Google (gtag) from the RSVP link once it exists.

> **No time, venue or seat claim runs before they are confirmed.** Phase 1 ads say "Wednesday,
> Nov. 18 · Fresno · Time and venue to be announced." Ads with a time, a venue or "a few seats
> left" wait for Phase 2 and for facts on file.

## 2. Meta (Facebook + Instagram)

- **Phase 1:** Leads objective, **Instant Form** "Get the Fresno dinner details first": name,
  email, phone (optional), company, two questions (deals in the last 12 months, primary model),
  and a consent line for email and SMS. The thank-you screen says time and venue come by email and
  offers the intro call. Leads sync to GoHighLevel via SHAI or Zapier. If you'd rather not use a
  form yet, send traffic to the intro call (calendly.com/vwhite-sundae/connect) instead.
- **Phase 2:** Sales or Leads objective → website conversions on the RSVP link, optimizing for the
  custom event `QualifiedApplication` once the form fires it (until then, `Lead`). Phase 1 leads
  get the invitation by email first, before the ads.
- **Special Ad Category check:** this is a business-opportunity event for investors, not a housing
  ad. If Meta flags it as Housing anyway, accept the category: it requires a 15-mile-plus radius
  and no age, gender or ZIP targeting. A 40-mile radius around Fresno covers Clovis, Madera and
  the surrounding Central Valley.
- **Audiences (one ad set per phase, Advantage+ audience with suggestions):**
  1. Custom audience: **Sundae Marketplace investor list, Fresno and Central Valley buy boxes**
     (hashed emails and phones).
  2. 1%-3% lookalike of marketplace buyers who closed a deal.
  3. Suggestions: real estate investing, wholesaling, house flipping, BiggerPockets, property
     management. Exclude: current members, Sundae employees, people who already requested a seat.
- **Placements:** Advantage+; 9:16 (Reels and Stories) and 4:5 (Feed) in Sundae's brand style.
- **Creative rotation:** Phase 1 = Reel 01 (save the date) · Reel 03 (harder market) · Image 01
  (save the date) · Image 02 (Josh). Primary text = captions C03 and C05. Phase 2 = the
  invitation creative made once the facts are confirmed, plus the Phase 1 winners.
- **Cut rule:** after $100 spent in Phase 1 or $150 in Phase 2, pause any ad with a CTR under
  0.8% or a cost per lead over $90.

## 3. LinkedIn

- **Audience:** Location = Fresno, Clovis, Madera and Visalia areas. (a) **Matched audience:** the
  marketplace list. (b) Job titles: Owner, Founder, CEO, Principal, Managing Partner,
  Acquisitions Manager or Director, Real Estate Investor; Industries: Real Estate, Investment
  Management, Leasing Real Estate; Seniority: Owner, Partner, CXO, Director; company size 2-200.
  LinkedIn audiences need at least 300 members to serve; if the Fresno audience is too small,
  widen the location to the Central Valley before widening the titles.
- **Phase 1:** single image (Image 06, 1200×627) with a LinkedIn Lead Gen Form (prefilled name,
  email and company, plus the same two questions as Meta).
- **Phase 2:** **Conversation Ad from Josh** (sender = Josh Stech): a personal invitation with two
  buttons, "Request my seat" (the RSVP link) and "Not this time"; plus Video 01 as Sponsored
  Content.
- **Bid:** maximum delivery for two days, then a cost cap of $120 a lead.

## 5. Retargeting (lower-cost seats)

- Meta: Phase 1 lead-form openers, people who watched 50%+ of any Fresno Reel, RSVP-link visitors,
  and Sundae Instagram and Facebook engagers (30 days) → the invitation creative, then the
  last-call Reel.
- LinkedIn: Lead Gen Form openers and video viewers (50%+) → Video 01 and the Conversation Ad.
- Frequency cap of about three a day; in a smaller market, check frequency every morning and
  refresh the creative when it passes about four a week.

## 7. Tracking and reporting (SHAI `/ads-metrics`, daily at 7 a.m.)

UTMs:

```
utm_source={meta|linkedin|google}&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=fresno-dinner-1118&utm_content={asset-id}
```

Report: spend · impressions · frequency · CTR · leads (Phase 1) · seat requests · qualified % ·
approved · confirmed · cost per approved seat · cost per attendee (after the event) · intro calls
attributed.

**Targets** (Manhattan Beach base model, assumptions): CTR over 1.0% on Meta, cost per qualified
request under $75, approved-to-attended over 70% and a paid cost per attendee of about $100.

## 8. Brand compliance (check every ad before it runs)

Page numbers cite Sundae's Creative Guidelines (Agency Partners, 2022), pp. 4-9, 15 and 30-31.
Items marked "studio spec" are the studio's working rules for applying the guide.

- **White space first:** white or light (#F4F7F9) fields, spacious margins and line height
  (pp. 4, 8-9). No dark overlays, gradients, glows or grain (studio spec).
- **Sundae red (#DB3D55) is represented** on every canvas (wordmark, "S" icon tile, accent bar,
  shape or highlight box) **but never as text** (pp. 4, 6, 30).
- **Type:** Merriweather headlines with real impact, Lato for everything else, copy in #4A4A4A;
  Lato Black for a headline when space is too tight for Merriweather (pp. 7-9).
- **Text on photos goes in highlight boxes** (white box with gray text, blue box with white text,
  or red box with large bold white text) (pp. 30-31).
- **Logo:** modest size, clear space at least the height of the "u" on every side (pp. 4-5).
- **Sized for an older audience,** with WCAG AA contrast; compliance or legal text is never
  smaller than the smallest other copy on the ad (pp. 4, 8).
- **Copy:** AP style (p. 15), as in "Wednesday, Nov. 18"; no superlatives such as "best" or
  "highest"; any offer or bid metric reads "An average of X" or carries an asterisk explaining it
  is an average (p. 4).
- **Photos:** the Fresno city photos are openly licensed (CC BY) and credited on the creative;
  they show the city, never the venue. Josh's photos come from sundae.com.
- **Franchise wording:** whether public ads may say "franchise," and Sundae's FDD and California
  registration status, are still open with Sundae's counsel. Until they answer, ads say
  "Membership" only.
