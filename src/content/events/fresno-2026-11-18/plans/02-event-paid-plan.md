## 2. Meta (Facebook + Instagram)

- **Objective:** Sales or Leads → website conversions on the registration page (once the pixel is on it); until
  then, landing-page views.
- **Special Ad Category check:** this is a business event for investors, not a housing ad. If Meta flags it as Housing,
  accept the category: it requires a 15-mile-plus radius and no age, gender or ZIP targeting. A 40-mile radius around
  Fresno covers Clovis, Madera and the surrounding Central Valley.
- **Audiences (one ad set, Advantage+ audience with suggestions):**
  1. Custom audience: **Sundae Marketplace investors with Fresno or Central Valley buy boxes** (hashed emails and phones).
  2. Deal Champs' list, if Stratton shares it with consent for ad matching (ask; never upload it otherwise).
  3. 1%-3% lookalike of marketplace buyers who closed a deal.
  4. Suggestions: real estate investing, wholesaling, house flipping, BiggerPockets, property management. Exclude
     current members, Sundae employees and people who already registered.
- **Placements:** Advantage+; 9:16 (Reels and Stories) and 4:5 (Feed).
- **Creative rotation:** Reel 01 (AI is changing real estate) · Reel 03 (a harder market) · Image 01, 01B or 01C (save
  your spot) · Image 02 (meet the speaker). Primary text = captions C03 and C05.
- **Cut rule:** after $150 spent, pause any ad with a CTR under 0.8% or a cost per registration over $90.

## 3. LinkedIn

- **Audience:** Fresno, Clovis, Madera and Visalia areas. (a) Matched audience: the marketplace list. (b) Job titles:
  Owner, Founder, CEO, Principal, Managing Partner, Acquisitions Manager or Director, Real Estate Investor; Industries:
  Real Estate, Investment Management; Seniority: Owner, Partner, CXO, Director; company size 2-200. LinkedIn needs at
  least 300 members to serve; widen the location to the Central Valley before widening the titles.
- **Formats:** single image (Image 06, 1200×627) to the registration page; **Conversation Ad from Josh** with two
  buttons, "Save my spot" (the registration page) and "Not this time"; Video 01 as Sponsored Content.
- **Bid:** maximum delivery for two days, then a cost cap of $120 a registration.

## 5. Retargeting

- Meta: registration-page visitors who didn't register, people who watched 50%+ of any Fresno Reel, and Sundae
  Instagram and Facebook engagers (30 days) → Image 03 (topics), then Reel 01.
- LinkedIn: page visitors (Insight Tag) and video viewers (50%+) → Video 01 and the Conversation Ad.
- Frequency cap of about three a day; in a smaller market, check frequency every morning and refresh the creative when
  it passes about four a week.

## 7. Tracking and reporting (SHAI `/ads-metrics`, daily at 7 a.m.)

```
utm_source={meta|linkedin|google}&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=fresno-meetup-1118&utm_content={asset-id}
```

Report: spend · impressions · frequency · CTR · registrations (HubSpot, by UTM) · cost per registration · attendees and
cost per attendee (after the event) · intro calls attributed.

**Targets** (Manhattan Beach base model, assumptions): CTR over 1.0% on Meta, cost per registration under $75, and
registrant-to-attendee over 60%.

- White or light fields, Sundae red on every canvas but never as text, Merriweather and Lato, copy in #4A4A4A, text on

  Creative Guidelines, pp. 4-9 and 30-31).
- AP style ("Wednesday, Nov. 18," "6-8:30 p.m."); no superlatives; any offer metric reads "An average of X."
- **Photos:** Fresno city photos (CC BY), credited on each asset; they show the city, not the venue. Josh's photos come
  from sundae.com. No Deal Champs logo or photo of Stratton is on file, so none is used.
- **Franchise wording:** whether public ads may say "franchise," and Sundae's FDD and California registration status,
  are still open with Sundae's counsel. Until they answer, ads say "Membership" only, and this event's ads stay about
