# Filming Gameplan — Fresno meetup, Wednesday, Nov. 18, 6-8:30 p.m.

**Purpose:** leave Fresno with footage and photos Sundae can use for months: company social accounts, the website, the
Membership VSL and paid ads. One evening should produce **30+ short clips, Josh's full conversation, one highlight film,
six to 10 interview soundbites and 100+ edited photos.**

> **The venue isn't on file.** Sundae's page sends the exact location to registered guests only. Everything below that
> doesn't depend on the room is ready (crew, audio, settings, interviews, releases, turnaround). Section 4 (positions)
> and section 5 (timings) are a template: finish them after a walk-through of the venue, and get written OK to film
> from the venue and from Deal Champs, who co-host.

**Kit (as planned for Sundae's events):** one or two cameras plus a phone as a vertical angle. Two wireless lav mics,
each paired to one camera, **both clipped to Josh's shirt**, while Josh speaks into the venue's handheld mic. Every
camera records clean voice audio, and the room hears Josh through the PA. If Stratton Brown speaks too, add a third lav
or hand Stratton the handheld and record the PA feed.

## 2. Audio plan (the most important part)

- **Lav 1 → A-Cam, Lav 2 → B-Cam**, both on Josh's shirt placket, 6 to 8 inches below the chin, cables hidden, mic heads
  up. Foam windscreens if any part is outdoors.
- **Handheld PA mic** at chest height, slightly off-axis; PA speakers **in front of and away from** Josh so the lavs
  don't pick up delayed PA sound.
- **Levels:** speech peaks around **-12 dB**, laughs under **-6 dB**; 32-bit float or a safety track if available, plus
  the transmitters' onboard recording as a backup.
- **Camera audio:** Ch1 = lav, Ch2 = the camera's onboard mic (room tone and applause).
- **Sync:** a hand clap before the conversation; 30 seconds of room tone before doors.
- **Interviews:** move Lav 2 to the guest.
- **Fresh batteries** before doors and again before Josh's conversation.

## 3. Camera settings (an evening event; adjust at the walk-through)

| | A-Cam (talk) | B-Cam (roam) | Phone |
|---|---|---|---|
| Resolution | 4K | 4K | 4K vertical |
| Frame rate | 24 or 30 fps (match all cameras) | 30 + 60 fps for slow motion | 30 fps |
| Shutter | 1/50 (24p) or 1/60 (30p) | double the frame rate | auto (lock exposure) |
| White balance | **Manual**, set to the room's lights, then locked | same as A-Cam | lock |
| Lens | 24-70 mm on a tripod | 35 mm and 85 mm primes, f/1.4-2.0 | 1× and 2× |

**Light Josh:** one battery LED panel with a softbox, matched to the room, 45° off Josh's spot, plus a small fill or
bounce. Frame Josh with any slides visible over a shoulder.

## 4. Camera positions (template — finish at the walk-through)

## 6. The interview station

Set it up in a quiet, well-lit spot; run it during the second networking block. Line up six to eight guests and Josh;
one to two minutes each, A-Cam medium + B-Cam close. **Get a signed release first.**

**Questions for guests:**
1. Who are you and what do you do? ("I'm ___, I run ___ in ___.")
2. How are you using AI in your business today, if at all?
3. Where are you finding seller opportunities right now?
4. What was the most useful thing you heard tonight?
5. In one sentence: why come to an evening like this?

**Questions for Josh (10-15 answers, 20-40 seconds each):**
1. How is AI changing real estate, and what does it mean for investors?
2. Where are seller opportunities emerging in today's market?
3. What are successful operators doing differently to acquire inventory and close deals?
4. Why Fresno, and why with Deal Champs?
5. What does Sundae bring to an operator that they can't build alone?

**Never** prompt or film earnings or income claims. If a guest volunteers numbers, flag the clip for legal review
before use (FDD Item 19 rules).
