# Filming Gameplan — the Sundae booth at SFR West

**Purpose:** photos and short clips from the booth for the recap post, the follow-up emails and future conference
posts. One person can do it with a phone.

**Kit:** a phone on a small gimbal, a wireless lav mic for interviews, a phone light, and a power bank.

## What to capture (each day, Dec. 7-9)
1. **The booth, empty and busy:** the backdrop straight on, then wide with people (consent first).
2. **Team at the booth:** a few photos of whoever is staffing it.
3. **Short interviews (15-30 seconds, with a signed release):** "What brought you to SFR West?" and "What's the
   hardest part of acquisitions right now?" Never prompt or film earnings or income claims; flag any volunteered
   numbers for legal review before use.
4. **Vertical clips for Stories:** a walk up to the booth, the QR code being scanned, the handout.

## Rules
- The conference and the hotel may have filming rules: check the exhibitor kit first.
- People on camera agree first; anyone who prefers not to appear is left out of the edit.
- Deliver 20 edited photos and three short clips by Thursday, Dec. 10, for the recap post (SHAI's video squad,
  `/new-edit`, can do the editing; each publish waits for approval).
