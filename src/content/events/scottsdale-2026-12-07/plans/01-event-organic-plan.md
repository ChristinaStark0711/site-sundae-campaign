# Event Plan — Organic: before, during and after SFR West

**Event:** Single Family Rental West (IMN), Dec. 7-9, 2026 (Monday to Wednesday) · Fairmont Scottsdale Princess,
Scottsdale, Arizona\
**Sundae's presence:** a 10×10 booth (booth number to come)\
**Goal:** conversations with experienced single-family rental owners, operators and investors at the booth → intro
calls booked for Sundae Membership.\
**Link and QR codes:** https://sundae.com/membership/

> The conference's own page lists 1,800+ SFR decision-makers, including 950+ owners and operators. Every target below
> is an assumption until Sundae has its own numbers from the booth.

## 2. During the conference (Monday, Dec. 7 → Wednesday, Dec. 9)

- **At the booth:** the backdrop, a stack of booth handouts (Flyer 01) and the QR code to sundae.com/membership.
- **Capture every conversation:** name, company, market, what they do (wholesale, flip, hold, acquisitions), and
  whether they'd like an intro call. A simple form or the team's CRM app on a phone; no paper lists left behind.
- **Stories each day:** the booth, the team, a short clip (Image 06, Reel 01), with consent from anyone on camera.

| When | Touch | Owner |
