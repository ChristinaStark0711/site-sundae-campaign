## 1. Who gets a seat (the qualification bar)

A seat is a sales opportunity. Fill the Courtyard with people who could become members,
not with spectators. Approve an application when **at least three** of these are true:

| Signal | Approve when | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Role | Owner, founder, principal or acquisitions lead of an investing company | The decision-maker on a membership |
| Volume | 6+ deals closed in the last 12 months (or 3+ with a funded team) | Proven operating experience (Sundae's own fit list) |
| Model | Wholesale, fix-and-flip, buy-and-hold acquisitions or a team doing several | The Sundae Engine fits acquisition businesses |
| Market | LA County, Orange County, Inland Empire, Ventura, South Bay | Territory relevance for this dinner |
| Marketing spend | $5,000+ a month on acquisition marketing today | Can deploy the system |
| Intent | "Scaling in the next six to 12 months" | Timeline for a decision |

Everyone else gets a polite "we'll invite you to the next one" plus the Sundae Marketplace
link. Nobody is left without a reply.

**Fix the RSVP friction first.** Today's RSVP Google Form requires a Google sign-in and asks
no qualifying questions. The new one-page RSVP funnel (link 3) asks the six questions above
in under 60 seconds, needs no login and sends each applicant to SHAI/GoHighLevel for
instant scoring and a same-hour reply.

## 4. Confirmation and show-rate system (free dinners lose 30%-40% to no-shows)

1. **Instant reply (under five minutes):** SHAI chat/SMS agent (AI-disclosed) thanks them and
   says a seat decision comes within two hours.
2. **Approval (under two hours):** email + SMS with calendar invite (.ics), parking/valet note
   and "reply YES to confirm."
3. **Personal call (Tuesday and Wednesday):** a human from Victoria's team, or the SHAI voice agent with AI
   disclosure, confirms attendance and asks "what's the main thing you want from the night?"
   That answer is logged for Josh's prep and seating.
4. **Reminders:** 24 hours, three hours and 30 minutes before the event, with the Shade address
   and a map link.
5. **Waitlist:** keep 15%-20% overbook headroom; release waitlist seats Wednesday at noon.
6. **Plus-ones:** allow a business partner (not a spouse/friend) — they apply in one tap.

Target: **70%+ of approved seats show** (base case: 71 approved → about 49 seated).
