# Always-On Membership (Franchise) Engine — Organic

**Goal:** a steady, *daily* flow of qualified operators asking to join Sundae Membership —
leads that cost nothing per click, built on Josh's authority, the 20,000+ investors already
on the Sundae Marketplace and a monthly city dinner roadshow.\
**Base-case target (projections tab):** 40 organic leads in month 1, rising to about 160 a month
by month 12 (about five a day), on top of paid.

> Positioning in one line: **"You know your market. We bring the systems to help you scale
> it."** (Sundae's own words.) Sundae is the national, seller-first brand. Members are the
> local operators who run it in their territory.

---

## 1. Five organic engines

### Engine 1 — The Marketplace advantage (Sundae's edge)
Sundae already has 20,000+ property investors bidding on its marketplace. Some of them are
exactly the operators Membership is built for.
- **Score the list monthly** (SHAI `/lead-list-builder` on the marketplace export, or the
  GoHighLevel import): activity (bids, closes), buy box, markets, company name. Top tier =
  active in a market with open territory.
- **Monthly "Operator's Outlook" email** — Josh's market data (the "Today's Outlook" slides)
  + one member story + next dinner invite → CTA "See if your market is open."
- **In-product prompts:** a Membership banner in the marketplace dashboard for buyers with
  10+ bids in 90 days. "Bidding on our deals? Run them in your market."
- **Investor Advisor scripts:** advisors already talk to buyers every day. Add one question:
  "Have you thought about running acquisitions under the Sundae brand?" Log it in GHL.

### Engine 2 — Founder-led LinkedIn (Josh)
Operators buy from operators. Josh's background — LendingHome founding partner, 1,200+
transactions at PBI, Stanford economics — is the credibility asset.
- **Cadence:** five posts a week plus 20 minutes a day of comments and DMs.
- **Content pillars** (rotate):
  1. **Market data** — "Today's Outlook": inventory, spreads, days on market, what changed.
  2. **Operator lessons** — acquisition math, speed-to-lead, conversion, dispositions.
  3. **Member spotlights** — with permission; no earnings claims beyond Item 19.
  4. **Behind Sundae** — the brand, the marketing machine (more than $100 million invested in
     reaching homeowners), the tech.
  5. **Dinners** — invite, recap, photos, quotes.
- **Monthly LinkedIn Live** (30 minutes): "Acquisitions in a harder market" with a member or an
  operator guest.
- **Newsletter:** "The Operator's Outlook" on LinkedIn — weekly, the same data as email.

### Engine 3 — Short-form + long-form video (Sundae company accounts)
- **Reels/Shorts/TikTok:** four or five a week, cut from dinner talks, LinkedIn Lives and member
  interviews (filming plan + SHAI's video squad). Hooks: "If you wholesale in {city}…",
  "The three numbers every acquisitions team should track", "Why we stopped…".
- **YouTube long-form:** one a week, 10-20 minutes. Dinner talks in full, member interviews
  ("How {member} runs acquisitions in Sacramento") and the "Operator's Outlook" monthly
  market video.
- Every video ends on one CTA: **"See if your market is open"** → membership application.

### Engine 4 — City dinner roadshow (one a month)
The Sacramento → Manhattan Beach format is a strong conversion asset for Sundae: a room of
qualified operators and Josh. Make it a monthly series.
- **Proposed order** (Sundae markets first): San Diego (November) · Las Vegas (December) · Orange
  County / Inland Empire (January) · Tampa (February) · Nashville (March) · Charleston (April) · Oklahoma
  City (May) · Salt Lake City (June) · Oakland/East Bay (July) · then expansion markets.
- Each dinner reuses this exact kit: RSVP funnel template, creatives, organic + paid plans,
  filming plan, follow-up. SHAI `/venue-finder` and `/restaurant-booking` shortlist venues.
- **Base model:** 40 guests a month × 3% → about 1.2 members a month from dinners alone,
  plus the content each dinner produces.

### Engine 5 — Search + answer engines (compounding)
- **Membership hub pages:** /membership plus one page per market — "Sundae Membership in
  Las Vegas" — with territory status, the local market data and FAQ.
- **Comparison and education content** (factual, sourced): "Real estate franchise vs.
  building your own acquisition engine", "What to look for in a real estate investing
  franchise", "How Sundae's marketplace connects deals with buyers".
- SHAI `/seo` + `/aeo`: FAQ schema, llms.txt, direct-answer paragraphs, so ChatGPT,
  Perplexity and Google AI Overviews cite Sundae when an operator asks "best real estate
  investing franchise" (the searcher's own words, not a claim to use in our copy).

## 2. Referral and partner channels
- **Member referrals:** members introduce operators from other markets. Recognition and any
  referral payment **must be cleared by franchise counsel** — some states regulate who may be
  paid for franchise sales (franchise broker/seller registration).
- **Partners:** hard-money lenders, title/escrow, transaction coordinators and CRM/dialer
  vendors talk to operators daily. Give them a co-branded "Operator's Outlook" to share.
- **Broker networks (optional, paid on close):** franchise broker networks (e.g., IFPG) bring
  pre-qualified candidates and charge a commission when a deal signs. Plan 04 covers them.

## 3. Outbound (optional, with care)
- **Public-records list of active operators:** LLCs that bought six or more SFRs in 12 months in each
  target market (from your property-data provider) → enrich → personal outreach from Victoria's
  team. SHAI `/lead-list-builder` + `/email-outreach` (Instantly with SPF/DKIM/DMARC,
  CAN-SPAM unsubscribe and postal address, sending pauses above a 0.3% complaint rate).
- **LinkedIn automation** (connection + DM sequences) works, but it breaks LinkedIn's terms
  and can restrict Josh's account. SHAI flags this and asks once. We recommend **manual DMs
  only from Josh's account**.
