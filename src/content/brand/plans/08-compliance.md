# Compliance Guardrails — read before anything goes live

This is a practical checklist, not legal advice. Sundae's franchise counsel has the final
word.

## Franchise sales
- **FTC Franchise Rule:** deliver the Franchise Disclosure Document (FDD) at least
  **14 calendar days** before a prospect signs anything binding or pays any money. No
  contracts or deposits at the dinner.
- **Financial performance representations:** no income, profit, deal-volume-per-member or
  "add a zero" *numbers* about what a member will earn unless they're in the FDD's **Item 19**,
  and then only as disclosed. "Add a zero to your real estate business" is Sundae's own
  headline — counsel should confirm it reads as aspiration, not an earnings claim, before
  it runs in paid ads. Every creative in this kit avoids specific earnings figures.
- **Registration states:** California (and several other states) require franchise
  registration before offering or selling, and regulate franchise advertising. **Confirm
  Sundae Membership's registration or exemption status in California before paid
  prospecting ads run.** Ask counsel whether any advertising filing is required first.
- **The word "franchise":** Sundae's public pages call the program "Sundae Membership."
  This kit uses Sundae's own language in public creative ("Membership," "territory,"
  "operators") and "franchise" in internal plans. Switch to "franchise" in public only when
  counsel approves.
- **Referral payments:** paying members, partners or brokers for franchise referrals can
  trigger franchise-seller/broker registration rules in some states. Counsel signs off on
  any referral program.

## Messaging (TCPA / CAN-SPAM / A2P)
- **SMS/calls:** only to people with prior express consent (marketing consent for
  promotional texts); honor STOP; quiet hours 8 a.m.-9 p.m. recipient time; A2P 10DLC-registered
  number; AI disclosure when an AI agent texts or calls. SHAI enforces these in code.
- **Email:** working unsubscribe, postal address, truthful subject lines. Marketplace
  emails go only to users whose terms/consent allow marketing.
- **Forms:** the RSVP funnel's consent checkbox is unchecked by default and separate for
  SMS vs. email.

## Ads platforms
- **Meta:** if an ad is classified as Housing (Special Ad Category), accept it — 15-mile+
  radius, no age/gender/ZIP targeting. No personal-attribute copy ("Are you struggling
  with…"). No before/after income claims.
- **Google:** no competitor trademarks in ad text; business-opportunity ads must be clear
  about what's offered.
- **LinkedIn:** Conversation Ads from Josh must come from his real profile with his
  permission.
- **Brand and claims (every ad):** no superlatives such as "best" or "highest"; any offer or
  bid metric reads "An average of X" or carries an asterisk; compliance text is never smaller
  than the smallest other copy. Full checklist: plan 02, section 8, and plan 04, section 7.
