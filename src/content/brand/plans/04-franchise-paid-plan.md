# Always-On Membership (Franchise) Engine — Paid

**Goal:** predictable daily lead flow of qualified operators into Victoria White's team's
calendar. **Base:** $25,000 a month in ads → about 227 paid leads a month at a $110 blended CPL, plus
organic → **about 11 leads a day** on average over 12 months → about 29 members in 12 months (about $10,000
in ad spend per member). Conservative ($15,000 a month) and aggressive ($40,000 a month) scenarios are on the
projections tab.

---

## 1. The funnel the ads drive into

1. **Ad** → Membership landing page / VSL → 60-second application (six questions).
2. **SHAI scores it instantly.** Qualified applicants book an intro call (calendar); everyone
   else goes to nurture.
3. **AI speed-to-lead** (a text in under 60 seconds, AI-disclosed) confirms and reminds.
4. **Intro call** (Victoria's team) → FDD delivered (14-day clock) → discovery/territory
   review.
5. **Signed member.**

**Application questions** (same scoring as the dinner): role · markets · deals closed in
12 months · primary model · monthly acquisition marketing spend · timeline to scale.
**Disqualify kindly:** new or aspiring investors go to the Sundae Marketplace and the
newsletter, not the sales calendar.

**Landing page essentials:** Josh's two- to three-minute VSL ("Stop building. Start scaling."), the
Sundae Engine (leads · conversion · profit), you bring / Sundae brings, proof points
($100M+ marketing investment, 20,000+ investors, national brand), territory map ("check your
market"), FAQ, application. SHAI `/vsl-script` writes the VSL and `/ghl-funnel` builds the
page in GoHighLevel. Or reuse the rebuilt site's /membership page (link 2).

## 3. Speed-to-lead (where franchise funnels often leak)
- **Under 60 seconds:** SHAI chat/SMS agent (GoHighLevel Conversation AI, AI-disclosed,
  consent-aware) texts the applicant, confirms two details, offers three call times.
- **Under five minutes:** qualified applicants get a call from the SHAI voice agent (AI-disclosed)
  or a human setter during business hours, with booking straight into Victoria's team's
  calendar.
- **No-show recovery:** reminders 24 hours, one hour and 10 minutes before the call; missed
  calls rebooked by text.
- **Long nurture:** "Operator's Outlook" weekly + next city dinner invite.

| Weeks | Test | Success metric |
|---|---|---|
| 1-2 | VSL page vs. short-form application page | Cost per qualified application |
| 3-4 | Founder talking-head vs. kinetic-type creative | CTR, hook rate, CPQL |
| 5-6 | LinkedIn Conversation Ad vs. Lead Gen Form | Cost per booked call |
| 7-8 | Dinner-invite offer vs. "check your market" offer | Booked-call rate |
| 9-12 | Scale the winners +20%/week while CPQL holds | Members/month, ad cost per member |

## 5. Budget guardrails
- **Scale rule:** +20% a week on any campaign whose cost per *held intro call* stays under
  $350.
- **Cut rule:** pause any ad over 2× target CPL after 1.5× target spend.
- **North-star efficiency:** ad cost per signed member of $12,000 or less (base model: about $10,300).
- **Daily ceiling:** SHAI enforces a daily spend ceiling across providers. Any increase asks
  Josh or Victoria first, by text.

## 6. Reporting
Daily (SHAI `/ads-metrics` → text at 7 a.m.): spend · leads · CPL · qualified % · calls booked.
Weekly (`/ads-optimize` recommendations, approval required): pause/scale proposals.
Monthly (`/forecasting`): leads → calls → FDDs → members vs. the projections, re-forecast.

## 7. Brand compliance (check every ad before it runs)

Page numbers cite Sundae's Creative Guidelines (Agency Partners, 2022), pp. 4-9, 15 and 30-31.
Items marked "studio spec" are the studio's working rules for applying the guide.

- **White space first:** white or light (#F4F7F9) fields, spacious margins and line height
  (pp. 4, 8-9). No dark overlays, gradients, glows or grain (studio spec).
- **Sundae red (#DB3D55) is represented** on every canvas (wordmark, "S" icon tile, accent bar,
  shape or highlight box) **but never as text** (pp. 4, 6, 30).
- **Type:** Merriweather headlines with real impact, Lato for everything else, copy in #4A4A4A;
  use Lato Black for a headline when space is too tight for Merriweather (pp. 7-9).
- **Text on photos goes in highlight boxes** (white box with gray text, blue box with white
  text or red box with large bold white text). A red hand-drawn circle is another way to bring
  in red (pp. 30-31).
- **Logo:** modest size, clear space at least the height of the "u" on every side, never
  crowded (pp. 4-5). Never stretched, recolored or placed directly on a busy photo (studio spec).
- **Sized for an older audience** (font size, line height and color) with WCAG-compliant
  contrast; compliance or legal text is never smaller than the smallest other copy on the ad
  (pp. 4, 8). Contrast is checked at the AA level (studio spec).
- **Copy:** AP style for dates, times and numbers (p. 15); no superlatives such as "best" or
  "highest"; any offer or bid metric reads "An average of X" or carries an asterisk explaining
  it is an average (p. 4). Proof points stay as Sundae publishes them, and earnings stay out
  (plan 08).
